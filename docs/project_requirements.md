# Project Requirements Summary

Source: `project.docx`

## Core modules
- Business profile (license, VAT, submission dates, contact)
- Taxes
- Customer group and customers (wholesale, retail, social media)
- Suppliers
- Courier partners (with delivery fee and settlement cycle days)
- Expense groups and expense types
- Receipt types
- Sales orders (items, delivery mode, courier fee)
- Sales (from sales order, tracking number)
- Delivery status (tracking and collection)
- Receipts
- Payments
- Day close summary
- Reports and dashboard
